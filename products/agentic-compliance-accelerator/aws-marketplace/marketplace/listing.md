# AWS Marketplace Listing

## Product name
Agentic Compliance Accelerator

## Subtitle
AI-powered compliance readiness and certification acceleration

## Short description
Agentic Compliance Accelerator helps organizations assess compliance gaps, organize evidence, generate documentation and coordinate remediation workflows using AI agents.

## Long description
Agentic Compliance Accelerator is an AI-powered compliance readiness platform from EmergentSoft Corporation designed to help organizations structure, accelerate and manage compliance preparation.

The platform connects compliance requirements with organizational information, evidence and remediation workflows. AI agents assist with assessment, gap identification, documentation generation and readiness activities, while maintaining a structured workflow for human review and organizational approval.

### Core capabilities
- Compliance readiness assessment
- Gap analysis
- Evidence organization
- Policy and documentation generation
- Remediation workflow support
- Compliance task tracking
- AI-assisted analysis and recommendations
- Multi-organization SaaS architecture
- Subscription and entitlement management
- Audit-oriented activity tracking

### Initial frameworks
- SOC 2
- ISO/IEC 27001

Additional frameworks may be added as the product expands.

### Workflow
1. Connect relevant organizational information and evidence.
2. Assess the current compliance posture.
3. Identify gaps against the selected framework.
4. Generate and organize required documentation.
5. Create remediation actions and track progress.
6. Prepare the organization for the formal certification or audit process.

Agentic Compliance Accelerator is a compliance-readiness and workflow platform. EmergentSoft does not represent itself as an independent certification body. Formal certification or attestation is performed by the applicable external certification or audit organization.

### AWS delivery
Agentic Compliance Accelerator is delivered as a SaaS solution. AWS infrastructure and services may provide the application hosting, storage, networking, security, monitoring and AI foundation depending on the deployment configuration.
