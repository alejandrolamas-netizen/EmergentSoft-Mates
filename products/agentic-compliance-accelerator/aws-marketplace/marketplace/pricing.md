# AWS Marketplace Pricing

## Commercial model
Initial recommendation: SaaS Contract.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Positioning |
|---|---:|---|
| Starter | Free | Basic compliance readiness |
| Professional | USD 299/month | Assessment, gap analysis, evidence and documentation |
| Business | USD 1,499/month | Advanced workflows, multiple workspaces, higher usage |
| Enterprise | Contact Sales | Custom commercial and enterprise requirements |

## Trial
Recommended: 14-day Professional trial.

## Private Offers
Use AWS Marketplace Private Offers for enterprise agreements, annual contracts and negotiated pricing.

## Metering
Do not introduce variable usage metering unless required by the final production billing model. Validate AWS Marketplace SaaS contract configuration before submission.

## Source architecture
The ECIA Phase 9 business specification defines Starter, Professional, Business and Enterprise entitlements and subscription transitions. Marketplace pricing must be reconciled with the actual production SaaS billing implementation before publication.
